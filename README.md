**CHAPTER 14 — PRODUCT CONTROL ACROSS THE BOARD**
*Consistency is the brand.*

**Tier 1 — Ingredient Standardization.** Centralize sourcing for flour, shortening, chicken, potatoes. Implement lot tracking from supplier to store. Require supplier scorecards with quarterly reviews.

**Tier 2 — Preparation Standardization.** Breading must be timed and temperature-controlled—not left to individual judgment. **BUSINESS ADVICE:** The margin on side dishes is often higher than on protein. Improving sides through integrated services (centralized sauce and base preparation) reduces waste and improves consistency.

**CHAPTER 20 — THE STORE MONITORING AGENT**
*The nervous system of the automated store.*

Every subsystem communicates through a generalized wireless protocol (MQTT or similar) to a central Store Monitoring Agent (SMA)—a small edge computer in the store's back office.

**Data Flow:** Sensors report to subsystem controllers (PLCs or microcontrollers) → publish to SMA via WiFi/Ethernet → SMA aggregates, timestamps, forwards to cloud → cloud dashboards display real-time status, alerts, historical trends → AI models analyze patterns to predict failures and optimize holding times.

**Key Metrics:** Uptime, temperature compliance, holding-time compliance, energy consumption (kWh/store/day), waste percentage.

**BUSINESS ADVICE:** The monitoring system is owned as a device robot by the company automation agent—corporate has visibility into every store's equipment status. This enables predictive maintenance, remote troubleshooting, performance benchmarking, and automated ordering.
